## The two parts of the system

1. **The public website** — what your customers see at your web address. Home page, product
   ("module") pages, solutions, pricing, blog, about, contact, and a "book a demo" form.
2. **The admin panel** — a private, password-protected area (add `/admin` to your web
   address). This is where your team edits almost everything on the public website and
   handles incoming enquiries. Nothing here is visible to the public.
