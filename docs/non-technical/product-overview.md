# Product overview

## What it is

The QTO ERP website is the public "shop window" for the QTO ERP software product. Its job is
to explain what QTO ERP does, show the individual tools ("modules") it offers, and turn
interested visitors into leads by getting them to **book a demo** or **contact sales**.

It is a marketing website, not the ERP software itself. Customers do not log in here to use
the ERP — they come here to learn about it and get in touch.

## What visitors can do

- Read about the product and the company.
- Browse the six ERP **modules** (Attendance, HR, Project Management, CRM, Salary, Finance)
  — each has its own detailed page.
- Browse **solutions** — the same product described for a particular type of business or
  team (for example HR teams, IT companies, call centres, construction, education).
- Read the **blog**.
- Fill in the **"Book a demo"** form.
- Use the **contact** form.
- Subscribe to the **newsletter**.

## What your team can do (in the admin panel)

Almost every piece of text, image, and list on the public website can be changed by your
team without a developer:

- Edit the home page (headline, statistics, customer logos, feature highlights, FAQs, calls
  to action).
- Create, edit, publish, and delete **blog posts** (with a full text editor and image
  uploads).
- Edit every **module** and **solution** page — headline, description, features, FAQs,
  testimonials, and more.
- Upload **demo videos**.
- Edit the **About**, **Contact**, and **Demo** pages.
- Edit navigation menus and the site footer.
- Read and reply to **demo requests** and **contact enquiries** by email, straight from the
  admin panel.
- Add and manage **staff accounts** with different permission levels.

## How it's built (one paragraph, no jargon)

The website runs on Cloudflare's global network, so it loads quickly from anywhere. All the
editable content lives in a database that the admin panel writes to. When your team saves a
change, the public page updates automatically within a short time — no developer or
re-deployment needed. Automatic emails (demo confirmations, contact
notifications, staff invitations) are sent through Cloudflare's email service.

## Who does what

| Role | Typical user | Can do |
|------|--------------|--------|
| **Super Admin** | Business owner / site manager | Everything, including adding/removing staff accounts |
| **Admin** | Marketing / content manager | Edit all content and settings, handle leads. Cannot manage staff accounts. |
| **Editor** | Content contributor | Create and edit content. Cannot delete content, change site settings, or manage staff. |

See [Managing leads & staff users](./managing-leads-and-users.md) for how to add people.
